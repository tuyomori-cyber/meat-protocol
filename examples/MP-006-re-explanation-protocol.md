# MP-006 — Re-explanation Protocol

## Situation

After changing assistants or starting a new session, a person rebuilds already-settled background, rationale, and constraints from memory.

## Current Flow

```text
Prior discussion and decisions
  ↓ reconstruct from memory
Human
  ↓ explain again
New session or system
```

## What the Human Decides

Which prior decisions remain relevant and whether they should be shared with the next context.

## What the Human Transports

The established context, reconstructed and retold because no portable, inspectable handoff exists.

## Why It Exists

Conversation state is often siloed, ephemeral, inaccessible, or too opaque to transfer safely.

## Possible Improvement

Offer an editable, user-owned handoff packet: decisions, constraints, sources, and uncertainty—rather than an invisible wholesale context transfer.

## Status

`Common`
