# MP-001 — Clipboard Relay

## Situation

A response, requirement, or research result from one system is copied into another system so work can continue there.

## Current Flow

```text
System A output
  ↓ Copy
Human
  ↓ Paste
System B input
```

## What the Human Decides

Which result is relevant and whether it is appropriate to share.

## What the Human Transports

The selected text, unchanged, through the clipboard.

## Why It Exists

The systems do not share context, access, or a deliberate handoff mechanism.

## Possible Improvement

Offer a user-approved “send selected result to…” handoff with scope preview, provenance, and editable context.

## Status

`Common`
