# Contributing

Thank you for helping name the tiny moments in which people become data mules.

## Add a protocol entry

1. Copy [`examples/TEMPLATE.md`](examples/TEMPLATE.md).
2. Assign the next available `MP-XXX` identifier.
3. Describe the situation before proposing a solution.
4. Separate what the person decides from what they only transport.
5. Name the current status and any reasonable improvement.

Keep examples specific, kind, and vendor-neutral where possible. This catalog documents UX patterns; it is not a leaderboard of products that made someone copy and paste.

## Good candidate test

An entry is usually a fit when all three are true:

- a person has already selected or understood the relevant thing;
- the next step is largely mechanical transfer of that thing or its reference; and
- a safe system-to-system path could reasonably replace that transfer.

Human review, editing, consent, and deliberate summarization are valuable work. Do not label them Meat Protocol merely because an AI is involved.

## Comics and assets

Put a comic in `comics/` and link it from the relevant entry. Include attribution and generation/editing notes in the entry when applicable.
