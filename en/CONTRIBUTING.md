# Contributing

Thank you for helping name the tiny moments in which people become data mules.

## Add a protocol entry

1. Copy [`examples/TEMPLATE.md`](examples/TEMPLATE.md).
2. Assign the next available `MP-XXX` identifier.
3. Describe the current workflow before proposing a solution.
4. Decompose the human roles in the path.
5. Identify any separable mechanical work; you do not need to settle the automation boundary.
6. Name the current status and any reasonable improvement.

Keep examples specific, kind, and vendor-neutral where possible. This catalog documents UX patterns; it is not a leaderboard of products that made someone copy and paste.

## Good candidate test

An entry is usually a fit when:

- a person is part of a system-to-system information or action path;
- their roles can be usefully described; and
- some portion may plausibly be separated into mechanical work or automation.

Routing, judgment, and approval may be valuable or intentionally human. Do not call a contributor’s work wasteful; decomposing the workflow is valuable even when the right boundary is unclear.

## Comics and assets

Put a comic in `comics/` and link it from the relevant entry. Include attribution and generation/editing notes in the entry when applicable.
